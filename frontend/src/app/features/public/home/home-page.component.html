Deliberate product scope

Focused on kennel operations.

Husky Tracking does not try to be a social network, GPS tracker, tour marketplace or accounting suite. It connects kennel state with the practical work of planning teams, recording actual activity and understanding workload.

Historical by design

See the kennel as it was, not only as it is.

Housing, class and operational state are date-aware, so historical views remain internally consistent.

Explainable by design

Rules stay visible to the user.

Role capability, availability, workload and pairing rules guide team building without opaque AI or hidden decision-making.

Built with real operational context

Software shaped by hands-on sled-dog experience.

Developed from sled-dog operations experience since 2019, including large-scale safari kennel work in Finnish Lapland. Explore the product safely through a complete fictional kennel.
