Anonymous demo workspace

Entering the interactive demo creates the strictly necessary ht_demo_session cookie, a random, opaque identifier. It contains no name, email address, or kennel information and is used only to keep your plans, teams, actual-work edits, notes, and temporary housing moves separate from other visitors. Reading this Privacy page does not create a workspace.

The cookie is HTTP-only, uses SameSite=Lax, and expires after the configured demo lifetime shown below. Scheduled cleanup removes expired workspace rows. Reset removes your mutable workspace data while preserving the shared fictional baseline. It does not delete the cookie or extend its existing expiry.

Contact form

If you choose to contact the project, the form asks for your name, email address, subject, message, and optional organisation so a reply is possible. The purpose is to respond to your request. Processing relies on the legitimate interest in communicating with visitors and, where genuinely applicable, steps you request before a possible contract.

Contact form messages are delivered through Brevo and are not stored in the Husky Tracking PostgreSQL database. Delivered correspondence may remain with the mail provider only as long as needed to handle the inquiry, reasonable follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. Please do not submit sensitive personal information.

Technical data, purposes, and legal bases

The application and its hosting layer necessarily process ordinary request data such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, requested paths, and error or security metadata. The purpose is reliable operation, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention, based on the legitimate interest in operating and protecting the service.

The anonymous identifier and temporary edits are processed to provide a functioning, isolated public demo, based on the legitimate interest in safely demonstrating the product. Husky Tracking does not include advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, browser fingerprinting, or behavioural analytics, and it does not sell personal information or use targeted advertising.

Only one strictly necessary demo cookie is used. The application intentionally uses no localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or marketing cookies. No consent banner is shown because no non-essential browser storage is used; that decision must be revisited if this changes.

Dog portraits

Opening a portrait displays the existing synthetic image from this site. It does not send photographs to an AI provider or add tracking, cookies or browser storage.

Recipients and international transfers

The application is hosted in the Netherlands (EEA). Technical request data may also be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers. Contact form messages are delivered through Brevo. Incoming email to huskytracking.com is handled by Cloudflare Email Routing before forwarding to the project owner.

Provider processing may involve other regions. We do not claim that all data remains in the EEA or make unverified transfer guarantees.

Retention

The anonymous workspace has the lifetime shown below. After expiry it cannot be reopened, and scheduled cleanup removes expired workspace rows. Contact correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the inquiry, reasonable follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. Technical request logs are kept for operational and security purposes under the applicable host and container log-rotation settings; no fixed retention duration is stated here.

Your choices and rights

You can reset your demo workspace at any time from the application sidebar. You may also delete the demo cookie in your browser; expired workspace data is cleaned automatically.

Where applicable, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal information. Not every right applies in every situation. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection supervisory authority for your circumstances. Contact the address below to ask a privacy question or exercise a right.
