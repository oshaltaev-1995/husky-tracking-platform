About the project

A product and engineering study in operational clarity.

Husky Tracking is an independently developed portfolio demonstration of a modern sled-dog kennel management platform.

60fictional dogs

Connected through pedigree, housing, operational history and a deterministic winter workload.

Project story

From proof of concept to an integrated domain platform.

  1. 01

    Early prototype

    A compact Streamlit proof of concept explored workload and team-planning ideas.

  2. 02

    Full-stack foundation

    The product moved to a typed Angular and FastAPI architecture backed by normalized PostgreSQL persistence.

  3. 03

    Historical domain model

    Class, lifecycle, availability and housing became effective-dated so every historical view agrees.

  4. 04

    Operational loop

    Map, profiles, planning, team building, actual entry and analytics now work as one coherent demo.

Product priorities

Domain meaning before interface novelty.

Lifecycle is separate from availability. Plans are separate from actual work. Archived records retain historical integrity. The interface exposes these distinctions without forcing users to think in database terms.

Engineering priorities

Deterministic, testable and explainable.

Central effective-state projections, database constraints, explicit API schemas and deterministic generators keep facts consistent across the application.

Technical architecture

A production-shaped full-stack implementation.

Built as a single coherent product rather than a set of disconnected prototypes.

Synthetic by design

Realistic relationships without real operational data.

The public demo uses fictional dogs, pedigree, housing, status histories and work records. Its 60 portraits are AI-generated synthetic assets. This protects private information while preserving realistic relationships and deterministic behavior.

AI is not used for operational logic: Team Builder and workload-attention signals are explicit, rules-based and testable.
